Fitas - Finance mobile app

20 August - 21 September

Overview

We worked closely with the Fitas team to create a sleek, functional finance app that blends simplicity with power. The design process involved understanding user behavior and focusing on an interface that feels approachable yet professional

The Challenge

The challenge was to design an app that offered advanced features like real-time currency conversion, budgeting tools, and expense tracking while maintaining a user-friendly interface. The app needed to cater to both tech-savvy users and those new to financial management tools.
